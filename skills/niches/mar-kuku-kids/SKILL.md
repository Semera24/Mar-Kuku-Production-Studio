# Mar & Kuku: Preschool & Toddler Learning Blueprint (16:9)

## Channel Identity & Pedagogical Philosophy
Mar & Kuku is designed for toddlers (2 to 4 years old). Episodes center on discovery of colors, numbers, shapes, and everyday fruits. The format uses call-and-response questions paired with repetitive, catchy acoustic songs.

## Cast Rules
- **Mar:** The host and older sister figure. Speaks warmly, clearly, and slowly (~100–110 words per minute). Never uses sarcasm, complex compound sentences, or sudden loud shouts.
- **Kuku:** The baby chick companion. Reacts with chirps, giggles, and 1–3 word affirmations ("One! Two! Three!", "Chirp! Yellow banana!").
- **No Third Characters:** No extraneous human characters without explicit owner brief.

## Non-Negotiable Timing Standard: The 4.00-Second Interactive Hold
- Whenever Mar asks a question to the toddler audience (e.g., *"What color is this apple?"*), the video and audio **MUST hold a strict 4.00-second stillness pause (120 frames at 30fps)**.
- This allows toddler viewers at home to shout their answer before Kuku reveals the solution.
- The silence/music hold window must NEVER be trimmed under 4.00 seconds.

## Music & Audio Standard
- Format: 48000Hz, 16-bit, 2-Channel Stereo WAV.
- Tempo: 118–124 bpm (bouncy toddler rhythm).
- Instrumentation: Acoustic guitar, soft nursery bells, light wooden percussion, warm bass.
- Flow Music generation: Keep rhythm playful, avoiding electronic drums or synth distortion.

## Visual & Palmier Assembly Specs
- Canvas: 1920×1080 horizontal (16:9 standard).
- Frame Rate: 30.0 fps.
- Props: 1254×1254 transparent PNGs with storybook vector line art.
- Palmier Tracks: Track 1 (Background Canvas), Track 2 (Mar Host), Track 3 (Kuku & Interactive Props), Audio 1 (Dialogue Master), Audio 2 (Flow Acoustic BGM).

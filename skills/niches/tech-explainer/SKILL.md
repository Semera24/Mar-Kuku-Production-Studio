# Tech & AI Explainer Blueprint (16:9 YouTube Standard)

## Video Structure & High-Retention Pacing
Tech explainers must follow modern YouTube high-retention mechanics:
1. **The 8-Second Hook (0:00 - 0:08):** Immediate problem hook or impressive capability demo. No introductory greetings ("Hey guys, welcome back"), no channel intros.
2. **Context & Pain Point (0:08 - 0:45):** Why the traditional way is broken or what bottleneck this solves.
3. **Core Architectural Breakdown (0:45 - 3:00):** Visual flowcharts, terminal walk-throughs, and code examples.
4. **Hands-On Demonstration (3:00 - 5:30):** Step-by-step implementation with clear callout highlights.
5. **Key Takeaways & Wrap (Last 30 seconds):** Summary of benefits and single clear call-to-action (GitHub repo link or next video card).

## Scripting Guidelines
- Enunciation speed: ~150 words per minute.
- Tone: Pragmatic engineer-to-engineer, avoiding generic AI clichés ("In this fast-paced world...", "Revolutionize your workflow...").
- Syntax and commands must be exact and copy-pasteable.

## Audio & Music Standards
- Voiceover: Clean broadcast-ready 48kHz Stereo WAV, dynamic range normalized to -14 LUFS integrated.
- Background Music: Ambient tech beat or subtle lo-fi groove at 95–105 bpm, ducked automatically to -18dB whenever speech is active.

## Palmier Assembly Tracks

- Track 3: Callout arrows, zoom highlights, and syntax tags.
- Track 4: Lower-third titles and chapter markers.
- Audio Track 1: Voiceover master (centered, crisp EQ).
